01 / ABOUT

Connecting perception
with understanding.

I am a Ph.D. student at The Chinese University of Hong Kong, Shenzhen and FNii, advised by Prof. Zhen Li and Prof. Shuguang Cui. My research focuses on lane topology reasoning and autonomous driving, including work with T Lab, Tencent.

I will join AutoMan Lab at Nanyang Technological University as a visiting Ph.D. student from October 2026 to October 2027, hosted by Prof. Chen Lv (吕辰) and supported by a CSC scholarship.

Previously, I received my master's degree from Sun Yat-sen University, working with Prof. Haifeng Hu and Dr. Weipeng Hu on heterogeneous face recognition and person re-identification.
