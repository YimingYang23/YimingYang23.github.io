Yiming Yang.

Ph.D. Candidate at CUHK-Shenzhen & FNii

I study how autonomous systems perceive road scenes, reason about lane connectivity, and model how the world evolves. My research spans lane topology reasoning, world models, and vision-language models.

I am advised by Prof. Zhen Li and Prof. Shuguang Cui, with research on autonomous driving in collaboration with T Lab, Tencent. Previously, I worked on cross-modal face recognition and person re-identification at Sun Yat-sen University with Prof. Haifeng Hu and Dr. Weipeng Hu.
